# Outline

### Who Is Missing School in Pittsburgh, and Are Attendance Gaps Getting Better or Worse?
 
This project explores how school attendance has changed over time in Pittsburgh Public Schools, which student groups experience the highest rates of chronic absenteeism, and how mentoring programs could potentially help improve school attendance.

## Initial sketches
> For my initial sketches I worked in 4 as follow:
> Sketch 1 — District Chronic Absence Trend Over Time: This line chart is intended to show the overall trajectory of chronic absenteeism across Pittsburgh Public Schools from the post-pandemic spike through the most recent school years, illustrating how absence rates have remained elevated.

> Sketch 2 — Chronic Absenteeism by Race Over Time: This multi-line chart breaks down absence trends across racial subgroups (African-American, Hispanic, Multiracial, Asian, and White) to identify which student groups are most affected and how disparities between groups persist over time.
