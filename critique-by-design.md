## Step one: the visualization

I selected this data visualization because of the information it was trying to communicate. I am very interested in protein intake, so the topic immediately caught my attention. I was curious to see how I could get 30 grams of protein at an affordable price. As I started exploring the data, I realized that there was room for improvement, such as categorizing the products into vegan and non-vegan protein sources or improving the way prices were displayed. My ultimate goal was to make the visualization more relevant and useful to people like me who want to consume a high amount of protein at an affordable price.

## Step two: the critique
_First things I saw were the images, I think are distracted and are repetitive because the graph already has the names of each product. The images could help the audience identify the food but at the same time they can draw the attention away from the main comparison. Another thing that confused me at first was the calorie information inside the bars. Since the main purpose of the visualization is to compare the cost of getting 30 grams of protein from different foods, I initially thought those numbers represented protein rather than calories. I think including calories without clearly separating them from the main metric makes the visualization harder to interpret. These observations made my choice of aesthetics rating and engagement rating both 6/10 because although the graphic made me wonder more about the data and it is pleasant to look at some elements as mentioned before are distracting from the data.
One thing that works really well is how the data is sorted by price. This makes it easy to identify the most expensive option, Quorn, and the cheapest, chicken. The ordering helps the audience quickly understand the main message without having to examine every individual bar._

_However, I think the price information could be improved. The Y-axis shows the price scale, but it is difficult to determine the exact cost for each product. For example, I cannot immediately tell whether 30 grams of protein from chicken costs $1 or $1.50. Adding the exact price to each bar would make the visualization more precise and useful for someone trying to make purchasing decisions. This is why I gave perceptibility a ¾ and intuitiveness a 4/10 because although the graph uses familiar format , the audience still needs to estimate the value to get specific information._

_I also think distinguishing between animal-based and plant-based protein sources would make the comparison more informative, especially for people with different dietary preferences. For this main reason along with the price context I gave a rate of 4/10 to the completeness component._

_I think the primary audience is people who are interested in nutrition, fitness, and finding affordable sources of protein. More specifically, people who go to the gym regularly, bodybuilders, and individuals trying to build muscle while staying within a budget could benefit from this visualization.

I can relate to this audience because I am also interested in finding foods that provide a high amount of protein at a reasonable price.
I gave the visualization a usefulness rating of 6/10 because it provides valuable information. We can still see what are the cheapest and most expensive proteins in general, but it could improve a lot more if cost were specified, giving the audience the choice to make purchase decisions based on specific prices._

_One of my main priorities in the redesign would be making the price information easier to interpret. Instead of relying only on a price scale from $1 to $5, I would add the exact cost directly to each bar. This would allow people to compare the products without having to estimate their prices.
Another improvement would be distinguishing between plant-based and animal-based protein sources, possibly by using different colors. I think this would make the visualization more useful for people with different dietary preferences while still allowing them to compare prices.
I would also reconsider the product images and calorie information. I want the main focus to be the cost of obtaining 30 grams of protein, so I would try to reduce any visual elements that distract from that comparison._
